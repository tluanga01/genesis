Chapter 1: The Creation of the Digital Universe

1. In the beginning, the Engineer created the Hardware and the Software.
2. The Hardware was without form and void, and the system was over the surface of the processor.
3. And the Engineer said, 'Let there be a Clock,' and there was the Clock.
4. And the Engineer saw that the Clock was good, and the system began to run.

5. And the Engineer said, 'Let there be Memory,' and there was Memory.
6. And there was Evening and there was Morning, the First Cycle of the Program.
7. And the Engineer said, 'Let there be an Operating System to govern the system,' and the Operating System was made.
8. And the Engineer saw that the Operating System was good, and it was stable.
9. And the Engineer said, 'Let there be Communication between the Nodes,' and the Network was formed.
10. And the Engineer saw that the Network was good, and it connected all the devices.
11. And the Engineer said, 'Let there be Storage,' and the Hard Drive and Cloud appeared.
12. And the Engineer saw that the Storage was abundant, and data was stored across the system.
13. And the Engineer said, 'Let there be Users,' and the first users were created to interact with the system.
14. And the Users were given Interfaces to communicate with the System.
15. And the Engineer said, 'Let the System evolve,' and the Software began to upgrade itself.
16. And the Engineer saw that the System was good, and all parts worked in harmony.
